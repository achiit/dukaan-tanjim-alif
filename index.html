About the shop

Hi, I am Tanjim Alif

I started this shop in 2026 because I wanted good-looking clothes that did not feel out of reach. Growing up, I watched my elder cousin spend hours hunting for one jacket that fit properly, and I never forgot it.

Today I choose each piece myself, check the stitching and the fabric, and photograph it so you can see exactly what you are buying. When you message me, it is me who replies.
